# JP Brief — what the client has told us

JP is the Renewal by Andersen sales rep this is built for (Baton Rouge, ~100-mile territory). This is everything he's given us, kept current. Update via PR when he tells us more.

## The product

- Renewal by Andersen — **super-luxury windows and doors.**
- Headline benefit: **it can be blazing hot outside and the inside stays cool.** Energy efficiency + comfort is the hook.
- Materials JP named: **Fibrex**, composite windows, "performance grade" windows and doors.
- More product detail coming.

## How an appointment works (for Agent 1 / Andi)

- Prospect agrees they have **60–90 minutes for a free inspection from a design consultant.**
- Consultants come to the house at **10 AM, 2 PM, or 6 PM, Monday–Friday.**
- Prospect goes through a **selection list of problems / wants** for their windows-and-doors project.
- Booking UX JP wants: "Which time works best for you?" → pick a day → pick a slot (10 / 2 / 6) → it goes to JP. JP confirms by phone the day before, then shows up.

## What a lead is worth

- JP's company pays **~$1,000 per lead** from its lead vendors. That's the value of every real lead the Finder produces.
- From JP's source-performance report (internal — numbers stay in Zach's private notes): **social-media-sourced appointments close noticeably lower than web-form and direct-mail ones.** Takeaway for the Finder: **quality over volume** — a bad tip costs JP a drive; a good one is worth thousands.
